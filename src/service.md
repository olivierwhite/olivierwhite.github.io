Alongside research and teaching, I coordinate international research evaluation, advise space agencies, edit journals and sit on ethics and university committees.

## International peer review

Since 2010 I have been Associate Science Officer for Peer Review with the European Science Foundation, coordinating research-funding selection across life sciences and engineering. This means identifying experts, building panels, defining criteria, checking conflicts of interest, running about eight panel meetings a year and quality-assuring the reports. To date I have contributed to the evaluation of more than 15,000 research projects, with €10–15 M allocated each year.

Partners include ESA, NASA, the AXA Research Fund, FWO (Belgium), FCT (Portugal), the Fight Kids Cancer foundations, the King Baudouin Foundation, the Compagnia di San Paolo, the Shota Rustaveli National Science Foundation (Georgia), the Graphene Flagship, EuroPlanet, the Luxembourg Institute of Health, and the universities of Naples, Turin, Eastern Piedmont, Ghent, Antwerp, UCLouvain, Lille, Grenoble, Bordeaux, Lorraine and Aix-Marseille.

Before that, as Science Officer in the ESF Space Sciences Unit (2009–2010), I ran the peer review of five ESA research announcements, including ISS experiments, bedrest, Concordia station, drop towers and parabolic flights, and worked on the THESEUS roadmap for European human space exploration.
