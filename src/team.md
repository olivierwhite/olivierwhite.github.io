## Master's and Bachelor's students

More than 50 Master's theses supervised in movement sciences, psychology, engineering and medicine.

## Joining the team

I welcome enquiries from students and postdocs with a background in engineering, physics, mathematics, movement sciences or psychology who want to work on motor control, altered gravity or movement variability. Please write with a CV and a short statement of interest.

External PhD juries: Aix-Marseille Université, UCLouvain, KU Leuven, Medical University of Graz, University of Munich, Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS.
