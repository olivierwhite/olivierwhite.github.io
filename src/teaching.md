## Approach

- **Measuring with what students carry.** Since 2021, about 450 first-year students measure movement with their smartphone sensors.
- **Project-based programming.** In engineering schools, students choose their own topics and work in pairs.
- **Learning by building.** Since 2025, five students from four programmes (engineering, psychology, medicine, ergonomics) have developed an instrumented device to measure hand function, from literature review to working prototype.
- **Assessment in the age of generative AI.** Individualised problems and personalised corrections generated per student in biomechanics, to limit copying while keeping marking fair.
- **AI literacy.** AI referent of the UFR STAPS since 2025; contributor to the CAIRE train-the-trainers initiative.
