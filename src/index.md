I study how the brain plans, controls and adapts movement, and how it copes when the environment changes. Trained as a computer-science engineer before a PhD in computational neuroscience, I combine human experiments with mathematical models of control, noise and variability.

My experiments take place in the lab, in immersive virtual reality and on haptic robots, but also in parabolic flights, human centrifuges and bedrest facilities. Changing gravity, or perturbing the senses, reveals what the nervous system normally predicts and compensates without our noticing.
